# Contact

The law requires a service like this to publish points of contact. The operator's identity and registry entry go on this page.

## Points of contact

- Point of contact for authorities (DSA Article 11): `authorities@wesupport-ai.com`
- Point of contact for recipients, including notifiers (DSA Article 12): `notice@wesupport-ai.com`
- Removal orders for terrorist content (Regulation (EU) 2021/784): `authorities@wesupport-ai.com`. The phone number for this contact goes to the registry and is not published on this site.
- Legal notices about public content: `/notice`
- Security reports: mail `notice@wesupport-ai.com`, the first contact `/.well-known/security.txt` names. The form at `/notice` is made for notices about public content: it asks where the content is and why it is illegal, and every notice it takes is closed with a public leaf that carries a salted hash of the notice and an outcome code. Do not send a security report through it.

These accept electronic communication in Greek and English. A person reads them: the same person who decides notices and orders. Mail to `authorities@wesupport-ai.com` and `notice@wesupport-ai.com` is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a Google mailbox; see `/legal/cloudflare-disclosure.md` and `/legal/retention.md`.
